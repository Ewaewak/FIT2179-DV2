Section 01 · The Nation

18 Clubs with One City Dominating.

The AFL is Australia's most attended and beloved sport, however one city dominates out of all. Melbourne alone hosts five clubs, three of the largest stadiums in Australia, and more than half of the league's total annual attendance. The map below shows every AFL home ground across Australia where Bubble size = average home crowd in 2024

AFL Home Grounds Across Australia — Average Home Crowd 2024

All 18 AFL clubs across Australia. Bubble size = average home crowd 2024. Colour = state. Hover for club, stadium and crowd details. Data: AFL Tables 2024.

The Melbourne cluster in the south east dominates immediately with five clubs within 5 km of each other, all drawing 35,000–65,000 fans per game. Perth's two clubs (WA, purple) draw well at Optus Stadium. By contrast, GWS Giants and Gold Coast Suns consistently struggle to fill their smaller grounds.

Why Melbourne Is Different

With every other city having one club, one stadium, and one train line.. Melbourne alone has nine clubs which shares 3 venues within a 1.5 km radius. The MCG stadium alone hosts 100,024 people which is more than any stadium in Australia and some cities stadiums combined.

Gold Coast and GWS Giants, the AFL's two newest clubs, sit at the bottom with averages under 15,000.

Section 02 · Melbourne's Precinct

Why Melbourne Dominates

With having passionate fans, Melbourne also has a very strong infrastructure in its stadiums since the three venues sit within a 1.5 km radius along the Yarra river and all being walking distrance of train stations. The MCG alone holds 100,024 people which is more than any stadium in Australia. There is also a train network designed around match days delivers fans directly to the gate.

Section 03 · The Match Day Surge

When the Crowd Arrives

Melbourne's train network is engineered around the MCG. On a normal Saturday, Jolimont station handles around 4,200 boardings. On an AFL match day, it hosts Over 28,500 which is a +580% surge that no other station in Australia experiences. Section 04 · Club by Club

Who Fills the Stadiums?

Not all clubs are equal. Collingwood and Carlton consistently fill the MCG to near capacity, while Docklands clubs struggle to attract half that number. This gap between Melbourne's clubs and the rest of the cities reflects on its decade long supporting culture and the unique advantage of playing at Australia's largest stadiums.

Section 05 · Network Health

Can the Network Keep Up?

Since the arrival of COVID-19, it decimated Melbourne's train patronage in 2020-2021. By 2024, the recovery is strong but not yet complete. Since the return of the AFL crowds to post pandemic levels, can Melbourne's train infrastructure handle it?
