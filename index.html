Section 01 · The Geography

Three Venues. One Network.

Melbourne's sports precinct is one of the most concentrated in the world. The MCG, AAMI Park, and Marvel Stadium sit within a 1.5 km arc along the Yarra. Every match day, hundreds of thousands of fans funnel through a handful of train stations. The map below shows the precinct — hover over each station to see its match-day boarding estimate and which train lines serve it.

Jolimont is the closest station to both the MCG and AAMI Park — a tiny commuter stop that becomes Melbourne's busiest micro-hub for 3 hours every match day, handling an estimated 28,500 boardings. Flinders Street, as the city's main interchange, absorbs the broadest fan flow across all lines.

Section 02 · The Precinct

Scale & Capacity

Understanding the pressure on Melbourne's train network starts with understanding the sheer scale of the precinct. The MCG alone can hold more people than the entire population of many regional towns — and when it's full, those 100,024 fans need to get home, mostly by train.

Section 05 · Network Health

Does the Network Hold Up?

COVID-19 devastated Melbourne's train patronage in 2020–2021. By 2024, recovery is strong but not yet complete. The question is: as patronage rebounds and match-day spikes intensify, can the infrastructure cope? The scatter below lets you explore the relationship between crowd size and estimated station boardings — drag to select a region.

Crowd Size vs Station Boardings — Is There a Relationship?
